# Debugging at Midnight

The screen glows blue at half past two,
the cursor blinks, a patient eye —
I've chased this error all night through
and still don't know the reason why.

A semicolon out of place,
a variable named twice in haste,
the logic wore a borrowed face
and all my clean assumptions, erased.

But then — a line I'd overlooked,
a null where something whole should be.
I fixed it, saved, and softly looked:
the terminal agreed with me.

I closed my laptop, dimmed the light.
The code would hold until the dawn.
Some things are only solved at night,
and some things break again by morn.
