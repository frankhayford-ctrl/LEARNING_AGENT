# The Space Between

Between the lines of written code,
a quiet logic finds its home —
each function like a winding road
that leads somewhere we've never known.

We build with words the way we dream,
in structures fragile, strong, and bright,
and nothing is quite what it seems
until it runs and works just right.

The bug is just a hidden door,
the error message, someone's clue —
we fix, commit, and push once more
and morning comes, and so do you.
